Objective
To identify why employees leave the company and understand which workplace factors and employee groups are associated with attrition.

Analysis & Key Findings
- Department-wise analysis shows that Sales has the highest attrition count among the displayed departments, followed by Research and Development.
- Job satisfaction analysis shows the highest number of exits in satisfaction level 3.
- Employees in the High experience category account for the largest share of exits among the displayed experience categories.
- Attrition counts are relatively close between employees who work overtime and those who do not.
- The dashboard enables interactive analysis by department, job role, and gender to support employee retention decisions.
