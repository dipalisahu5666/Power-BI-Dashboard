Objective
To evaluate supply chain performance and identify suitable transportation routes by comparing cost, quantity, defect rates, and inspection outcomes.

Analysis & Key Findings
- Route B has the highest total cost, followed by Route A and Route C, making cost efficiency an important factor in route selection.
- Road transportation handles the highest quantity among the displayed transportation modes.
- Healthcare has the highest average defect rate among the displayed product types, followed by Skincare and Cosmetics.
- Inspection analysis shows more items in Pending and Fail categories than in Pass, highlighting the need for quality-control improvements.
- Location-wise cost and quantity comparisons help identify differences in operational performance across locations.

The dashboard supports route selection by bringing together cost, volume, and quality indicators rather than relying on cost alone.
